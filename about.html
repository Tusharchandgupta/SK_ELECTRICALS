About SK Electricals

SK Electricals is a family-run electronics & electrical appliances store located on Shree Ram Janki Mandir Road in Siswa Bazar, Maharajganj (Uttar Pradesh). Founded and run by Shailendra Gupta, we focus on reliable products, clear pricing, and honest repair work.

Our mission

To provide the people of Maharajganj with dependable electrical products and fast, trustworthy servicing so they can run their homes and businesses without interruptions.

What we sell

  • Inverters (all major brands) and batteries
  • Voltage stabilizers — domestic and industrial
  • Weighing scales & calibration services
  • Electronic components — PCB cards, inverter motherboards, stabilizer boards
  • Small home appliances — mixers, irons, LED bulbs, chargers

Why locals trust us

  • Fast, reliable repairs and honest service
  • Knowledgeable staff with years of hands-on experience
  • Local presence — easy to visit and get after-sales support
