Inverter Motherboard & PCB Diagnostics

Complete component-level troubleshooting for Luminous, Microtek, Eastman, Sukam, and Exide inverters. We diagnose faulty MOSFETs, damaged drivers, blown fuses, and charging circuit failures with precise oscilloscope & multi-meter testing.

✓ Common fixes: Overload tripping, battery not charging, humming noise, dead display.
🔌

Voltage Stabilizer Repair & Calibration

Repairing automatic voltage regulators (AVR), mainline copper stabilizers, and dedicated AC stabilizers from V-Guard, Microtek, and Premier. We replace worn-out relay switches, calibrate cut-off voltage thresholds, and replace damaged step-up transformers.

✓ High/Low voltage cut-off tuning · Copper rewinding support.
⚖️

Weighing Scale Calibration & Certification

Certified calibration for counter retail scales, 100kg/200kg platform scales, and mandi heavy-duty balances. Sensor load cell replacement, zero-drift error correction, motherboard tuning, and government verification stamping assistance.

✓ Standard test weights used · Accurate down to 1 gram.
🔋

Tubular Battery Health Check & Acid Testing

Extend battery lifespan by up to 2 additional years. Specific gravity measurement with precision hydrometer, terminal corrosion cleanup, desulfation charging, and distilled acid water top-up services.

✓ Instant battery backup load testing on bench.
🏡

On-Site Emergency Visit & Installation

Cannot bring your heavy double-battery inverter or heavy platform scale to the shop? Our technician visits homes, hospitals, and commercial shops across Siswa Bazar and nearby villages for on-site fault isolation and installation.

✓ Rapid response in Siswa Bazar, Nichlaul, and Ghughli.
☀️

Solar Inverter & Rooftop Maintenance

Health checks for solar charge controllers (PWM & MPPT), solar panel wiring, earthing verification, and hybrid solar PCU programming to maximize solar power absorption and eliminate electric bills.

✓ Certified solar hybrid system checks.
